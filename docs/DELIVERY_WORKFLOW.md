# Delivery workflow

Delivery Composer maps five familiar consulting activities to real application state:

1. **Identify** — capture the environment, outcome, and client need.
2. **Define** — structure roles, capabilities, clearance, and timing.
3. **Execute** — apply constraints and compose candidate options.
4. **Assure** — inspect evidence, scores, trade-offs, and exceptions.
5. **Learn** — record approval or actionable revision feedback.

The workflow is intentionally cyclical. A revision creates a new linked evaluation instead of mutating the prior result, allowing reviewers to inspect what changed and why.

## Governance boundary

- The server validates briefs, reviews, and candidate overrides.
- Clearance and availability are gates, not hidden score components.
- Review notes stored with a revision become the authoritative input to re-evaluation.
- Demo feedback is temporary and never described as autonomous learning.
- The final decision remains with a human delivery leader.
